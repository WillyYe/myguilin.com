Welcome to Guilin

Guilin, in Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, is celebrated for the most beautiful landscapes in China. Its emerald karst peaks, the meandering Li River, and misty terraced fields have inspired poets and painters for over a thousand years — immortalized in the saying "桂林山水甲天下" (Guilin's scenery is the best under heaven).

As Guilin's #1 inbound travel team, we've partnered with 100+ hotels and local agencies across the region, welcoming over 100,000 international visitors each year. Whether you seek nature, slow travel, or cultural immersion — we craft experiences that match your pace.

Top Attractions in Guilin

Seven must-see destinations — from the iconic Elephant Trunk Hill to bamboo rafts on the misty Yulong River.

City Icon

Elephant Trunk Hill

The symbol of Guilin — a limestone hill shaped like an elephant dipping its trunk into the Li River. Most magical at sunrise when morning mist rises from the water.

The Crown Jewel

Li River (漓江)

An 83 km ribbon of jade-green water between Guilin and Yangshuo, lined with surreal karst peaks. The cruise past the "20-yuan" viewpoint is the most photographed scene in China.

Underground Wonder

Reed Flute Cave

A 180-million-year-old limestone cave filled with multicolored stalactites, stone pillars, and an underground lake — dramatically lit to feel like a natural palace.

Vibrant Town

Yangshuo & West Street

A laid-back riverside town wrapped in peaks. West Street blends cafes, bars, and local life — the backpacker heart of Guilin and gateway to the countryside.

Hidden Gem

Longji Rice Terraces

Zigzag terraces carved into mountain slopes by Zhuang & Yao minorities over 650 years. Mirror-like fields — breathtaking in spring flood and golden autumn.

Must-Do

Yulong River Rafting

Glide down the emerald Yulong River on a traditional bamboo raft, pushed by a local boatman through misty karst peaks and lush bamboo forests — the most serene and photogenic experience in Yangshuo, ranked #1 by international visitors.

Night View

Two Rivers & Four Lakes

A network of lakes and rivers around the city, best seen on an evening cruise past the illuminated Sun & Moon Pagodas reflected in still water. Pure romance.

Pedal through emerald rice fields and under towering peaks on flat country roads around Yangshuo. The most romantic, free-spirited way to feel Guilin's countryside.
