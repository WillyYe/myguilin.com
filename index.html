Getting Here

Multiple ways to reach Guilin from anywhere in the world

View detailed map →
✈️

By Plane

Guilin Liangjiang International Airport (KWL), with flights to major Chinese cities and international hubs.

🚄

By Train (High-Speed)

Guilin Station, North Station & West Station on the high-speed rail network. ~2.5 hrs from Guangzhou.

🚌

By Bus

Long-distance buses connect Guilin to Yangshuo (~1.5 hrs), Longji Terraces, and nearby cities.

🚗

By Car / Taxi

Well-connected highway network. Taxis and ride-sharing apps widely available throughout the city.
