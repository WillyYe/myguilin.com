The Practical Guide

Why Guilin?

⛰

If you're Googling "best places to visit in China" or scrolling through travel forums asking "is Guilin worth it?" — here's what every seasoned traveler will tell you.

Guilin is not just scenery. It's one of the few places in China where you can arrive with zero Chinese language skills and still navigate comfortably. English-friendly hotels are concentrated in Yangshuo West Street, most tour operators speak fluent English, and the city has direct flights from Hong Kong, Seoul, Singapore, Bangkok, and major mainland hubs. You don't need a guide for everything — but having one unlocks experiences you'd never find on TripAdvisor.

Best time to visit: April–October, with peak golden light in September–October (harvest season at Longji Terraces) and misty magic from March–May. Summer is lush but humid; winter is quiet, cheaper, and surprisingly mild (rarely below 5°C). The Li River looks completely different in each season — many visitors come back twice.

Recommended stay: 4–6 days minimum. 2 days for the Li River cruise & Yangshuo countryside, 1 day for Longji Terraces, 1–2 days flexible for cycling, cormorant fishing, or a side trip to the rice noodle capital of China.

Top Attractions in Guilin

Eight must-see destinations — ranked by popularity among our 100K+ guests.

🏞️ Epic

Li River Cruise

Guilin to Yangshuo — 83 km of karst peaks and river bends, including the famous scenery behind the 20-yuan banknote.
🐘 Iconic

Elephant Trunk Hill

Guilin's city symbol — a limestone arch shaped like an elephant drinking from the river, glowing at night.
🎉 Lively

Yangshuo West Street

Pedestrian street packed with cafes, bars, and shops — the social heart of Guilin's foreign traveler scene.
🌾 Breathtaking

Longji Rice Terraces

Cascading dragon-back terraces carved into mountainsides — a mirror of sky during water season.
🪨 Mystical

Reed Flute Cave

A limestone cave filled with colorful stalactites and stalagmites — nature's underground palace.
🚣 Serene

Yulong River Rafting

Gentle bamboo raft rides through countryside karst — quieter and more peaceful than the main Li River.
🌉 Romantic

Two Rivers & Four Lakes

A scenic chain of waterways through Guilin city center — stunning by day, magical illuminated at night.
🏘️ Timeless

Xingping Ancient Town

The backdrop of China's 20-yuan banknote — historic riverside town with Ming-Dynasty streets and iconic karst vistas.

Experiences You Can't Miss

Eight classic ways to connect with Guilin's soul — the experiences every foreign traveler remembers.

NATURE LOVER

Li River Cruise

Glide past legendary karst peaks on the famous 4-hour scenic route — the #1 Guilin experience.

ACTIVE

Countryside Cycling

Pedal through emerald rice fields and karst peaks on flat riverside trails around Yangshuo.

ADVENTURE

Longji Terraces Trek

Hike among mirror-like rice terraces and minority villages — soft adventure with sunrise sea-of-clouds views.

CULTURE

Cormorant Fishing

Watch fishermen and trained cormorants catch fish by firelight at dusk — a centuries-old tradition.

SERENE

Bamboo Rafting

Drift on a bamboo raft through crystal-clear waters between intimate karst peaks — quieter than the cruise.

THRILLING

Scale world-class limestone karst towers — Yangshuo is Asia's top sport-climbing destination.

AUTHENTIC

Cooking Class

Shop a local market, then cook beer fish and rice noodles with English-speaking chefs in Yangshuo.

SCENIC

Sunrise Viewpoint

Climb to the panoramic overlook at dawn for layered karsts glowing gold above the sea of clouds.

Taste Guilin

Food & Local Flavors

Guilin's kitchen is bold, sour, and fragrant — here are the dishes every visitor should try.

Beer Fish (啤酒鱼)

Yangshuo's signature — fresh river fish braised in beer with tomatoes and peppers. Best eaten along West Street.

Guilin Rice Noodles (桂林米粉)

The city's beloved breakfast — silky noodles in savory broth with fried peanuts, scallions, and chili oil. Eat standing up, like a local.

Lipu Taro Pork (荔浦芋扣肉)

A festive classic — layers of taro and pork belly steamed until meltingly soft, sweet and savory at once.
