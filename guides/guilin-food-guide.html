Guilin Food Guide: 12 Dishes to Try & Where to Eat

2026-04-02· 7 min read· Visit Guilin Local Team
Food Local eats Yangshuo

Guilin's food is subtle, sour, and rice-forward — quieter than Sichuan heat or Cantonese sweetness, but addictive once you tune in. This is a practical eat-list, not a restaurant directory: the dishes worth chasing, and the neighborhoods where locals actually queue.

The 12 dishes

  1. Guilin rice noodles (桂林米粉): the breakfast of the city — thin rice noodles in a fragrant broth with peanuts, pickles, and your choice of braised meat. Eat standing up at a corner shop like a local.
  2. Beer fish (啤酒鱼): Yangshuo's signature — river fish braised in beer with tomatoes and chili. Best on West Street side lanes, not the main strip.
  3. Stuffed tofu / stuffed snails (酿): the "niang" technique — tofu, eggplant, or river snails stuffed with pork. A regional specialty.
  4. Osmanthus treats (桂花): the city's flower appears in sweet soups, jelly, and tea cakes. Try osmanthus liangfen (cold jelly).
  5. Lipu taro pork (荔浦芋扣肉): steamed layers of taro and pork belly — rich, festive, from nearby Lipu county.
  6. Bamboo rice (竹筒饭): rice steamed inside a bamboo tube, smoky and fluffy — common around Longji villages.
  7. Cured pork & mountain veg: the highland version of comfort food near the terraces.
  8. Guilin chili sauce (桂林辣椒酱): a fermented staple — buy a jar at the market, not the airport.
  9. River shrimp & snails: small, sweet, best at a riverside stall.
  10. Oil tea (油茶): a salty, savory tea whipped with ginger and rice — an acquired, very local habit.
  11. Sticky rice in lotus leaf: a portable, fragrant lunch.
  12. Sweet fermented rice balls (酒酿圆子): a warm dessert soup for cool evenings.

Where locals actually eat

  • Guilin city: morning rice-noodle shops near the train station and along the riverside; the night market for snacks.
  • Yangshuo: West Street side alleys for beer fish; the riverside for river shrimp.
  • Longji: your guesthouse kitchen — the homestay meal is the experience.

Dietary notes

Vegetarians do fine on noodles, tofu, and mountain veg, but broths often contain meat — say so clearly. Our local team can flag vegetarian-friendly stops when we plan your route.

One rule

Follow the queue. A shop with a line of scooters at 7 a.m. is serving better noodles than any glossy restaurant with a tripadvisor plaque. The food in Guilin rewards the unpretentious.
