Getting to Guilin 🗺️

Multiple convenient ways to reach one of China's most beautiful destinations

✈️ By Plane — Guilin Liangjiang Airport

IATA Code: KWL

The airport is located about 25 km from downtown Guilin (~30 minutes by taxi or ~45 minutes by airport bus). It offers direct flights to major Chinese cities including Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Chengdu, Xi'an, and Hangzhou.

Taxi: ¥60–80 | Airport Bus: ¥20 (runs to city center every 30 minutes)

🚄 By High-Speed Train

Guilin is well-connected on China's high-speed rail network with three stations:

• Guilin Station (桂林站) — closest to city center
• Guilin North (桂林北) — main HSR station
• Guilin West (桂林西) — newer station

From Guangzhou South: ~2.5 hrs | From Changsha: ~3 hrs | From Guiyang: ~2 hrs

🚌 By Bus / Coach

South Bus Station (汽车南站) serves routes to Yangshuo (~1.5 hrs, ¥30), Longji Rice Terraces (~2.5 hrs, ¥35), and nearby cities like Liuzhou and Nanning.

Buses run frequently during daytime hours. For Yangshuo, you can also take direct buses from the airport.

🚗 By Car / Taxi / Rideshare

Guilin is connected by expressways to Nanning (G72), Guangzhou (G65/G78), and other major cities. The road network within Guilin and surrounding areas is excellent.

Ride-sharing apps (Didi Chuxing) are widely available. Taxis are metered and affordable for short trips around the city.
